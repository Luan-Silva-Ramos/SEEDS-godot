# AI-Assisted Production Disclosure

The product architecture, market comparison, procedural animation code, documentation, and quality-control workflow were developed with generative-AI assistance through ChatGPT.

The customer PNG frames were rendered deterministically with Python, NumPy, and Pillow. No external stock texture, third-party reference image, franchise, protected character, logo, trademarked visual language, named-artist imitation, or proprietary font was used in the customer overlay frames.

Human production work included product selection, effect taxonomy, technical constraints, seed design, motion review, loop metrics, light/dark compositing review, sampler selection, packaging, licensing, storefront preparation, and final QA.
